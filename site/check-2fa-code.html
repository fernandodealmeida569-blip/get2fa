What the result means

  • Matches the current code: your key and app are fine. If a site still rejects it, the site may have a different key (2FA was reset) or the code expired while typing.
  • Matches an older or newer code: one of the clocks is off. Turn on automatic date and time on the phone that runs the authenticator.
  • No match: the key belongs to another account, was copied incompletely, or the site uses 8 digits, 60 seconds or SHA-256.

How the clock check works

We compare your device's time with the time reported by our server in a normal page request. TOTP codes change every 30 seconds, so a difference of more than a few seconds is enough to make codes fail.
