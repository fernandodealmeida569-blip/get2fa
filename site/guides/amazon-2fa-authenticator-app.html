How to set up Amazon Two-Step Verification with an authenticator app

Amazon calls 2FA “Two-Step Verification” (2SV). Using an authenticator app is safer than text messages and works without cell signal. Here's how to set it up.

Updated 2026-11-23

Step by step (computer)

  1. Sign in to Amazon, open Account & Lists and choose Your Account.
  2. Click Login & security.
  3. Next to Two-Step Verification (2SV) Settings, click Edit (or Turn on), then Get Started.
  4. Select Authenticator App. Amazon shows a QR code; use the link below it to see the key as text if you want to save it.
  5. Add the key to your authenticator app (or paste it into Get2FA), type the 6-digit code into Amazon and click Verify OTP and continue.
  6. Add a backup method when asked. Amazon requires one, usually a phone number.
  7. Click Got it. Turn on Two-Step Verification to finish.

The Amazon Shopping app reaches the same Login & security page, so the steps are the same on iPhone and Android.

Switching from text messages to an app

If you already use 2SV with SMS, go to Login & security → Two-Step Verification (2SV) Settings → Edit, add an authenticator app and set it as your preferred method.

One account, all Amazon sites

Your Amazon login is also used by Audible, Prime Video and other Amazon services, so the same 2SV protects all of them.

Keep a copy of the key

Before you scan the QR code, look for the option to see the key as text and save it in your password manager. If your phone is lost or replaced, you can paste that key into Get2FA or a new authenticator app and get your codes back in seconds. Never share the key or a screenshot of the QR code: anyone who has it can generate your codes.
