How to set up Coinbase 2FA with an authenticator app

Coinbase requires 2-step verification and supports any TOTP authenticator app, along with passkeys, security keys and push prompts. Here's how to add an authenticator app.

Updated 2026-11-19

Step by step

  1. Install an authenticator app on your phone (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Duo, 2FAS…).
  2. Sign in to Coinbase from a web browser. You can't change your 2-step method from the Coinbase mobile app.
  3. Open the security settings page and select the 2FA settings tab.
  4. Under Available methods, click Set up next to Authenticator app. You'll be asked to confirm with your current method.
  5. Scan the QR code or copy the key, then enter the 6-digit code to finish.

Which 2FA method is best on Coinbase?

Coinbase lists security keys and passkeys as its strongest methods and lets you turn on several methods at once as backups. Your account is only as secure as the weakest method you leave on, so if you add an authenticator app, consider turning off text messages.

Extra care for crypto accounts

  • Coinbase may ask for a code when you sign in from a new device or send crypto, so keep your app (or saved key) handy.
  • Store the key offline or in a password manager, never in your email or photos.
  • No real Coinbase employee will ever ask for your 2FA code.

Keep a copy of the key

Before you scan the QR code, look for the option to see the key as text and save it in your password manager. If your phone is lost or replaced, you can paste that key into Get2FA or a new authenticator app and get your codes back in seconds. Never share the key or a screenshot of the QR code: anyone who has it can generate your codes.
