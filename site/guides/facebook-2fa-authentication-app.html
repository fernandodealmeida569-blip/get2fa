How to set up Facebook 2FA with an authenticator app

Facebook supports text messages, security keys and authenticator apps for 2FA. An app is safer than SMS and keeps working without signal. Here's how to set it up and keep the key.

Updated 2026-11-19

On a computer

  1. Sign in at facebook.com and click your profile picture in the top right.
  2. Go to Settings & privacy → Settings.
  3. Open Accounts Center, then Password and security.
  4. Click Two-factor authentication and choose your Facebook account.
  5. Choose Authentication app.
  6. Facebook shows a QR code and a key with a Copy key button. Copy and save the key.
  7. Add it to your authenticator app, or paste it into Get2FA, and type the 6-digit code to confirm.

On the app (Android or iPhone)

Open the menu (or your profile picture on iPhone), then Settings & privacy → Settings → Accounts Center → Password and security → Two-factor authentication. Pick the account, choose Authentication app and tap Copy key.

Save the 10 recovery codes

Once 2FA is on, Facebook can give you 10 recovery codes. Each one works once, in place of an authenticator code. Find them later in Password and security → Two-factor authentication. Store them with your key, outside your phone.

Why keep the key?

If your phone is lost, stolen or reset, the key lets you get your codes back in minutes on a new device or on Get2FA, without going through Facebook's account recovery.

If the option isn't there

Facebook moved 2FA into Accounts Center. If you don't see it, update the app or open facebook.com/settings in a browser. You can only add a 2FA method while you still have access to the account; if you're locked out, you'll need Facebook's recovery process.
