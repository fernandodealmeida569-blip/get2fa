How to transfer Google Authenticator to a new phone

There are two ways to move your codes: sign in to Google so they sync, or export them with a QR code. Get2FA can also read that export QR and show each secret key.

Updated 2026-11-19

Option 1: cloud sync (easiest)

  1. On the old phone, open Google Authenticator and tap your profile icon.
  2. Sign in with your Google Account and turn on sync. A small cloud icon confirms the backup.
  3. On the new phone, install Google Authenticator and sign in with the same Google Account. Your codes appear automatically.

Sync is convenient, but your 2FA keys are then stored in your Google Account. Protect that account well.

Option 2: Export accounts (offline)

  1. On the old phone, open the menu and go to Transfer accounts → Export accounts.
  2. Select the accounts and tap Next. The app shows one or more QR codes.
  3. On the new phone, go to Transfer accounts → Import accounts → Scan QR code and scan each code.

The export works between Android and iPhone in both directions.

Get the secret keys from the export QR

The export QR code uses a special format (otpauth-migration://) that other apps often can't read. Get2FA can: take a screenshot of the export QR (on a phone you trust), then paste or drop the image on Get2FA. Each account appears with its name and live code, and the list shows the full otpauth:// link with the key. Save them in your password manager.

Everything is decoded in your browser; the image and the keys are never uploaded. Delete the screenshot afterwards.

Don't wipe the old phone too soon

Test a few logins on the new phone before resetting the old one. If something goes wrong after the old phone is gone, you'll need each site's backup codes or recovery process.
