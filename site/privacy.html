Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

The short version

Get2FA generates two-factor codes entirely in your browser. Your secret keys, codes and account names are never sent to our servers, stored, or shared with anyone.

What we don't collect

We do not collect the 2FA secret keys, otpauth links, QR code images or account lines you enter. They are processed in your browser's memory and are discarded when you close or reload the page. Links in the form get2fa.online/#KEY keep the key after the # sign, a part of the address that browsers do not send to servers.

What is stored on your device

We save two preferences in your browser's local storage: your light/dark theme and whether auto-copy is turned on. You can clear them at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Hosting and logs

Like any website, our hosting provider may record standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. These logs never contain your secret keys. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first service without cookies, to count page views; it does not read what you type.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Brazil), we ask for your consent before using advertising cookies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and Brazil's LGPD), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data and to withdraw consent. Because we don't collect your keys, there is no key data to request. For anything else, contact us.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@get2fa.online.
