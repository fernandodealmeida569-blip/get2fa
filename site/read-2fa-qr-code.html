Read a 2FA QR code and get the secret key

Drop, paste or upload a screenshot of a 2FA QR code. You'll get the secret key, issuer, account and settings, even from Google Authenticator export codes.

Drop a QR code image hereor click to choose a file · or press Ctrl+V to paste a screenshot
The image is decoded on your device and is never uploaded.

When is this useful?

  • You have a screenshot of a 2FA QR code and want to save the key in a password manager.
  • You exported accounts from Google Authenticator and want the actual keys, not just a transfer to another phone.
  • You're a developer checking what your app's QR code really contains.

Google Authenticator export codes

The Transfer accounts → Export accounts screen makes QR codes in a special otpauth-migration:// format that holds several accounts. This reader decodes it and lists each account with its key. See how to transfer Google Authenticator.

Privacy

Decoding uses your browser's built-in barcode reader or an open-source library loaded from this site. Delete the screenshot when you're done: anyone with it can generate your codes.
